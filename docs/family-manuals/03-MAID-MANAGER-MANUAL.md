## The Pacing Feature (Why This App Exists)

**Your body has limits. The app knows them.**

### Setting Up Your Pace Profile (Do This First)

When you first open the app, you'll see "Set Pace Limits." This is critical.

**Fill in:**
- **Max hours per day:** How many hours can you actually work?
- **Max jobs per day:** How many houses can you handle?
- **Min break between jobs:** Recovery time (driving doesn't count)
- **High-pain days:** Reduce all numbers by 50%

**Why this matters:** The app will **warn you** when you're about to overbook. Yellow = caution. Red = stop.

## Adding a Job (Two Ways)

### Method 1: The Smart Way (Plan Ahead)

1. Tap "+ Add Job"
2. Enter:
   - **Client name:** "Johnson"
   - **Address:** (type or use voice)
   - **Estimated time:** How long the job takes you
   - **Frequency:** Weekly? Monthly? One-time?
3. App calculates:
   - Drive time from previous job
   - Whether this fits your daily limits
   - If you're over-scheduling
4. **If the bar turns red:** The app is telling you "this is too much for today"

### Method 2: Emergency Add (Same-Day)

1. Tap "Emergency Add" (big red button — hard to miss)
2. Enter minimal info
3. App **immediately shows** if you're exceeding limits
4. **Yellow warning:** "This will put you at 6 hours. Continue?"
5. **Red warning:** "This exceeds your daily joint budget. RESCHEDULE?"

## The Pain Log (Important)

At the end of each job, the app asks: **"How are your joints?"**

### The Pain Scale (1 = Best, 10 = Worst):

| Level | Color | Meaning | App Response |
|-------|-------|---------|--------------|
| **1-3** | 🟢 Green | Feeling good, minimal pain | Normal pace, full schedule |
| **4-6** | 🟡 Yellow | Sore, some discomfort | Suggests shorter day tomorrow |
| **7-10** | 🔴 Red | High pain, struggling | Suggests canceling next job |

**Examples:**
- **1** = "I feel great, bring on the work"
- **5** = "My knees are aching but I can finish"
- **10** = "I can barely walk, need to stop"

**Why this matters:** The app learns your patterns. If Thursdays always hurt, it will suggest lighter Thursday loads.

## Troubleshooting for Carrie

### "The app says I'm over my limit but I feel fine"
**Solution:** Tap "Override" (you're allowed). But consider: the app might be seeing a pattern you don't. Maybe check your pain log from last week.

### "I forgot to hit 'complete' on a job"
**Solution:**
1. Find the job
2. Tap "Edit"
3. Set status to "Completed"
4. Enter approximate time
5. Save

### "My pain level is high but I need the money"
**Solution:** The app has a "Push Through" mode. It will:
- Let you book the job
- Mark it red
- Add extra break time after
- Suggest rescheduling tomorrow's first job

### "Voice doesn't understand client names"
**Solution:** Give clients short codes in your head.
- "Johnson house" → "House J"
- "Smith condo" → "Condo S"
- Or just type it — typing is fine too

### "I can't find tomorrow's schedule"
**Solution:**
1. Check the date filter at top
2. Tap "Tomorrow" button
3. Or swipe left to change day
