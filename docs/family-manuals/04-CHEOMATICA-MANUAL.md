## Welcome, Christyn

This app replaces typing out color formulas with sore hands, digging through notebooks, and trying to remember what you mixed for that client six months ago.

**What it does:**
- **Voice-first:** Speak your formulas, never type them
- Stores client color history forever
- Remembers developer volumes, processing times, custom mixes
- Works offline — your formulas are always on your phone

**What you need:**
- Your phone
- Willingness to talk to your phone (feels weird for 2 days, then natural)
- About 20 minutes to learn voice commands

---

## The Problem This Solves

**Old way:**
- Client asks: "What did you use last time?"
- You think: "Was it 6N with 20 vol? Or 5N with 30? And did I add gold?"
- You dig through a notebook
- Your hands hurt from writing all day

**New way:**
- Client asks: "What did you use last time?"
- You tap their name
- Exact formula appears
- You read it aloud or play the voice recording

**Your hands don't hurt. Your client is happy. You look professional.**

## The Two Ways to Use This App

### Method 1: Voice (Recommended — Saves Your Hands)

**Tap 🎤, wait for green light, speak:**

#### Adding a Client:
- "New client Sarah Johnson"
- "Add client with phone five-five-five-one-two-three-four"

#### Recording a Formula:
- "For Sarah: six N natural with twenty volume, thirty minutes"
- "Formula: seven G gold, mix with thirty volume, process twenty minutes"
- "Custom mix: half six N, half seven G, twenty volume"

#### Finding Info:
- "What did I use for Sarah?"
- "Show Sarah's history"
- "When was Sarah's last visit?"

#### Recording Notes:
- "Note for Sarah: resistant gray, bump up developer next time"
- "Sarah prefers cooler tones, avoid gold"

---

### Method 2: Tap (When Voice Isn't Working)

**For when the salon is too loud or voice is acting up:**

1. Tap "+ New Client" or pick existing client
2. Tap "Add Formula"
3. Use the **color wheel picker** (big, easy to tap)
4. Select developer volume (10 | 20 | 30 | 40) — big buttons
5. Set processing time (slider, works with thumb)
6. Tap "Save"

**Everything is designed for thumbs that hurt. Big targets. No tiny keyboards.**

## The Color Wheel (When Voice Won't Work)

Sometimes the salon is too loud. Sometimes you prefer to pick visually. That's fine.

### How to Use:
1. Tap "Add Formula"
2. **Big color wheel appears** — tap the general area (reds, browns, blondes)
3. **Specific colors appear** — tap the exact shade
4. **Level selector** (1-10) — big buttons, easy to tap
5. **Tone selector** (N, G, B, R, etc.) — swipe through
6. **Developer buttons** — 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 (can't miss them)
7. **Time slider** — drag with thumb

**Everything requires gross motor movement, not fine typing.**

## Custom Mixes (The Fancy Stuff)

When you mix two colors together:

**Voice:**
- "Formula: half six N, half seven G, twenty volume"
- "Mix: two parts seven N, one part eight G, thirty volume"
- "Custom: six N with pinch of red gold, twenty volume"

6. Set ratio (50/50, 2:1, etc.)
7. Save

## Allergies & Alerts (Critical Safety Feature)

**When a client has a sensitivity:**

1. Go to client card
2. Tap "⚠️ Alert" button (big and red)
3. Say or type the allergy
4. **Red banner appears** on their card forever

**Next time you open their card:**
- 🚨 Red banner: "ALLERGIC TO PPD — USE ALTERNATIVE"
- You cannot miss it
- You cannot forget it

**Voice command:**
- "Alert: [client] allergic to [product]"
- "Warning: [client] sensitive scalp, use twenty volume max"

## Troubleshooting for Christyn

### "Voice doesn't understand '6N'"
**Solutions:**
1. Say "six N" (with space)
2. Say "level six natural"
3. Say "six natural" 
4. If all else fails: Tap the color wheel

### "The salon is too loud for voice"
**Solution:** Tap mode. Everything works with big buttons. Or step into the back room for voice.

### "I forgot to record a formula"
**Solution:**
1. Find the client
2. Tap "Add Formula"
3. Record now (even if hair is done)
4. Add note: "From memory — check next time"

### "My hands are too shaky to tap"
**Solution:** Voice is better for you. Try voice in quiet moments, or ask someone to tap for you while you speak.

### "The app didn't save my recording"
**Solution:** This is rare, but:
1. Check client's history — might be there
2. If truly lost: Re-record immediately while memory is fresh
3. Add note: "Re-recorded same day"

## 🐛 Something Broken? Use the Bug Button

**Don't struggle. Tap the ladybug.**

At the bottom of every screen, next to the 🎤 voice button, there's a 🐛 bug button.

### When to Use It:
- Voice won't record your formula
- Client's color history disappeared
- Error message you don't understand
- App acting weird

### How:
1. **Tap 🐛** (big red ladybug button)
2. **Talk** — say what went wrong in your own words
3. **Answer 3 questions** — what you were doing, what you expected, what happened
4. **Done** — Will gets everything he needs automatically

**What it captures:** Your voice description, which client you were working with, the technical details, and a screenshot if you want.

**You don't need to know what's wrong. Just describe what you see.**
