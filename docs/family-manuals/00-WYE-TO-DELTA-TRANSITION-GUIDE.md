## 🚦 The Three Phases of Learning

### PHASE 1: Survival Mode (Week 1)
**Goal:** Open your app without panicking.

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Bookmark your app's URL on your phone
- [ ] Tap the icon 5 times until it feels normal
- [ ] Learn one button: "Add New" (whatever that means for your app)
- [ ] Practice: Add one fake thing, then delete it

**Success Metric:** You can get in and out without calling Will.

---

### PHASE 2: Functional Mode (Weeks 2-3)
**Goal:** Do real work with the app.

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Enter 10 real items/jobs/recipes
- [ ] Try the "voice" button (if your app has one)
- [ ] Learn what "Home" vs "Business" mode means
- [ ] Find the "Help" or "?" button (it's there somewhere)

**Success Metric:** You used the app for a real job today.

---

### PHASE 3: Mastery Mode (Month 2+)
**Goal:** Teach someone else.

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Show Mama how you use your app
- [ ] Set up your own "voice commands"
- [ ] Use the app when the internet is down (it works!)
- [ ] Never think about "the cloud" again

**Success Metric:** You forgot this used to be scary.

## 🆘 When Things Go Wrong

### "The screen is blank"
1. Wait 10 seconds (really, count to 10)
2. Pull down from the top (refresh)
3. Close the app, reopen it
4. If still broken: Text Will the URL that's on your screen

### "I can't log in"
1. You don't "log in" — this isn't Facebook
2. Just open the URL. That's it. No password on your device.
3. If it asks for a password, you're on the wrong site

### "My data disappeared"
1. It didn't. It's on your phone.
2. Check if you're in "Home" or "Business" mode (top of screen)
3. Toggle the switch — your data is there, just filtered
4. If still missing: Text Will with what you were doing

### "The buttons are too small"
1. Tap the accessibility menu (three lines or gear icon)
2. Turn on "Large Touch Targets"
3. Everything gets bigger. Your fingers thank you.

### "It's too confusing"
1. Put the phone down
2. Take three deep breaths
3. Open the app again — it's less scary the second time
4. Call Will. This is what family mesh is for.

## 📶 The Magic: Works Without Internet

**This is the DELTA difference.**

Your app stores data on YOUR phone first. When the internet is available, it syncs to the family mesh. When it's not, you keep working.

### What You Can Do Offline:
- ✅ Add items
- ✅ Edit records
- ✅ View everything
- ✅ Use voice commands

### What Needs Internet:
- 🔄 Syncing to other family members
- 🔄 Backup to the "Ark" (the home server)
- 🔄 Voice commands that need the cloud

### How to Tell If You're Offline:
- A small "Offline Mode" badge appears (usually top right)
- The app looks exactly the same
- Everything still works

**This is normal. This is the point.**
