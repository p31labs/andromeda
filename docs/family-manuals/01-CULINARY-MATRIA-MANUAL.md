# 🍳 Culinary Matria
## The Kitchen Command Center
### For Mama — Meal Prep, Scaling & Recipe Management

## Welcome, Mama

This app replaces your notebooks, mental math, and "oh no I need to feed 12 mechanics" panic.

**What it does:**
- Scales any recipe from "feed 4" to "feed 50" automatically
- Tracks what's in your home pantry AND your shop pantry separately
- Remembers what the mechanics actually ate (and liked)
- Works in your kitchen even with bad cell service

**What you need:**
- Your phone or tablet
- The recipes you already know (we'll put them in here)
- About 20 minutes to learn the basics

---

## The Two Modes (This Is Important)

At the top of the screen, there's a toggle:

### 🏠 HOME Mode
- Recipes for family dinners
- Home pantry tracking
- Smaller batches (4-8 people)

### 🏢 BUSINESS Mode  
- Recipes for the mechanics lunch program
- Shop pantry tracking
- Big batches (12-50 people)

**How to use:** Tap the toggle. The screen changes color. That's it.

---

## The Main Screen

When you open the app, you'll see:

### Recipe Cards
Each card shows:
- Recipe name (big text)
- How long it takes (prep + cook)
- Base servings (what the recipe was written for)
- **BIG BUTTONS:** "Cook for 4" "Cook for 8" "Cook for 12" "Cook for 15"

### How to Use the Buttons
1. Find the recipe you want (scroll or search)
2. Tap how many people you're feeding
3. The app opens a **Session** — this is your active cooking guide

---

## The Session Screen (Where the Magic Happens)

After you tap "Cook for X", you'll see:

### Ingredients Checklist
- Every ingredient needed, scaled to your batch size
- Checkboxes next to each item
- Tap to check off as you gather

### Progress Bar
- Shows how many ingredients you've checked
- Turns green as you complete

### At the Bottom:
- **✓ Complete Session** (only works when all ingredients checked)
- **Cancel** (if you change your mind)

---

## Voice Commands (The Fancy Part)

Tap the **🎤 Voice** button at the bottom. Speak clearly. Wait for the green light.

### Commands That Work:

**Adding to Pantry:**
- "Add six eggs to the home pantry"
- "Add two pounds of rice to the business pantry"
- "Add a dozen onions to the shop"

**Starting to Cook:**
- "Start the lentil soup for twelve people"
- "Make chicken rice for the mechanics, feed twenty"
- "Start scrambled eggs for eight"

**Checking Inventory:**
- "Do we have enough onions for dinner?"
- "How many eggs in the home pantry?"
- "What's in the shop pantry?"

**Subtracting (When You Use Stuff):**
- "We used a dozen eggs for breakfast" (subtracts 12)
- "Used two pounds of rice for the mechanics lunch"

**Shopping List:**
- "Add rice to the shopping list"
- "We're out of chicken broth, get ten cups"

## How to Add a New Recipe

**Method 1: Voice (Easiest)**
1. Tap 🎤 Voice button
2. Say: "Add recipe: [name]. Ingredients: [list]. Serves: [number]"
3. The app will create it (may need Will to verify)

**Method 2: Manual**
1. Find "Add Recipe" button (usually + or "New")
2. Fill in:
   - Recipe name
   - Description (optional)
   - How many it serves (base amount)
   - Prep time
   - Cook time
   - Tags (like "vegan", "quick", "mechanic-favorite")
3. Add ingredients one by one
4. Save

## Understanding Pantry Tracking

### What the App Tracks:
- **Home Pantry:** What's in your kitchen cabinets
- **Business Pantry:** What's at the shop for the mechanics

### How It Works:
1. You tell the app "Add 6 eggs" — it remembers
2. You use eggs — you say "Used 6 eggs" — it subtracts
3. The app shows you how many you have left

### Why This Helps:
- No more buying rice when you have 10 pounds
- No more "do we have enough onions?" stress
- Shopping lists based on what you actually need

## Troubleshooting for Mama

### "My recipes are gone!"
**Solution:** Check the Home/Business toggle. Recipes are filtered by mode. They're not gone, just filtered.

### "The app says 'no ingredients'"
**Solution:** You started a session but the recipe needs ingredients added. Tap "Edit Recipe" and add ingredients.

### "Voice commands don't work"
**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet (voice needs the cloud)
2. Speak slower
3. Try simpler words ("Add eggs" instead of "I would like to add some eggs please")
4. The voice button turns red if it didn't understand — just try again

### "The numbers look wrong"
**Solution:** The app scales recipes mathematically. Sometimes you need to round in your head. "2.3 cups" means "about 2 and 1/3 cups."

### "I don't see the recipe I need"
**Solution:** Add it! Or text Will the recipe and he'll add it for you.

## Emergency Mode: No Internet

**If the internet is down, the app still works.**

### What You Can Do Offline:
- ✅ View all recipes
- ✅ Start cooking sessions
- ✅ Use checklists
- ✅ See pantry counts (from last sync)

### What You CAN'T Do Offline:

### When Internet Returns:
- The app syncs automatically
- Your data appears on other devices
- Nothing is lost

## 🐛 Something Broken? Use the Bug Button

**Don't struggle. Tap the ladybug.**

At the bottom of every screen, next to the 🎤 voice button, there's a 🐛 bug button.

### When to Use It:
- Recipe won't scale
- Error message you don't understand  
- Pantry numbers look wrong
- Anything acting weird

### How:
1. **Tap 🐛** (big red ladybug button)
2. **Talk** — say what went wrong in your own words
3. **Answer 3 questions** — what you were doing, what you expected, what happened
4. **Done** — Will gets everything he needs automatically

**What it captures:** Your voice description, what screen you were on, the technical details, and a screenshot if you want.

**You don't need to know what's wrong. Just describe what you see.**
