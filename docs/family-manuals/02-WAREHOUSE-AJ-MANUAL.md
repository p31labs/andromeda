## Welcome, AJ

This app replaces clipboards, spreadsheets, and walking around the warehouse trying to remember what's where.

**What it does:**
- Scan QR codes on furniture (zero typing)
- Track which zone items are in
- Know what sold and what hasn't
- Work offline in the warehouse (no cell service needed)

**What you need:**
- Your phone
- The thermal label printer (for new items)
- About 15 minutes to learn the scan workflow

---

## The Big Idea: Zones

Your warehouse is split into numbered zones. The app tracks everything by zone.

**Zone 1-3:** Receiving (new stuff comes in)  
**Zone 4-6:** Cleaning/Prep (being fixed up)  
**Zone 7-8:** Photo/Listing (ready to sell)  
**Zone 9:** Sold/Staging (waiting for pickup)

**On the app:** Each zone has a colored badge. Tap a zone to see only that zone's items.

---

## The Three Actions (Everything Is One of These)

### 1️⃣ RECEIVE — New Item Coming In
**When:** A couch/chair/table arrives at the warehouse

**Steps:**
1. Tap "Receive New Item" button
2. Point camera at the auto-generated QR code (on your phone)
3. Tap the QR to a sticker (peel and stick to furniture)
4. Select: Item type (couch, chair, table, etc.)
5. Tap: What zone it's going to
6. Done. Item is in the system.

**Voice shortcut:** "Receive couch to zone two"

---

### 2️⃣ MOVE — Item Changes Location
**When:** You move something from one zone to another

**Steps:**
1. Tap "Move Item"
2. Scan the QR code on the furniture (with your camera)
3. Select: New zone
4. Done.

**Voice shortcut:** "Move to zone five"

---

### 3️⃣ SELL — Item Goes to Customer
**When:** Someone buys something

**Steps:**
1. Tap "Sell Item"
2. Scan the QR code
3. Enter: Sale price (what they actually paid)
4. Enter: Customer name/phone (optional but recommended)
5. Done. Item moves to Zone 9 automatically.

**Voice shortcut:** "Sold for two hundred"

## QR Code Workflow

### Printing New QR Codes:
1. When you receive an item, the app generates a QR
2. Tap the QR on your screen to make it big
3. Point thermal printer at it
4. Peel sticker and stick to furniture

### Scanning Existing QR Codes:
1. Tap "Scan" button
2. Point camera at the QR on the furniture
3. Hold steady until it beeps
4. Item details appear

**Pro tip:** If QR is damaged, you can type the item ID manually. It's printed under the QR as letters/numbers.

### Thermal Printer Troubleshooting:

**"Printer won't connect"**
1. Check printer power (green light on?)
2. Check Bluetooth on your phone (Settings → Bluetooth → Printer should show "Connected")
3. If not connected: Tap printer name in Bluetooth list
4. Still not working? Restart printer (hold power button 5 seconds, then turn back on)

**"Paper jam"**
1. Open printer lid
2. Remove jammed paper
3. Reload paper roll (shiny side up)
4. Close lid firmly

**"Prints are faded/blurry"**
1. Paper roll might be old/wet
2. Replace with new thermal paper
3. Wipe printer lens with soft cloth

**"I don't have the printer with me"**
- Screenshot the QR code on your phone
- Show the screenshot to the item
- Stick a regular label with the item ID written on it
- Print proper QR later when you have the printer

## Troubleshooting for AJ

### "The QR won't scan"
**Solutions:**
1. Make sure lighting is decent (turn on warehouse lights)
2. Clean the QR sticker if dirty
3. Hold phone steady for 2 seconds
4. If totally damaged: Tap "Manual Entry" and type the ID

### "I moved something but it's still showing old zone"
**Solution:** Pull down to refresh. The app updates when it syncs.

### "I received an item but don't see it"
**Solutions:**
1. Check zone filter — you might be looking at Zone 5 but item went to Zone 2
2. Pull down to refresh
3. Check "All Zones" view

### "The voice button doesn't understand 'couch'"
**Solution:** Voice needs internet. If warehouse has no signal, use buttons instead.

### "I need to change the price on something"
**Solution:**
1. Scan the item
2. Tap "Edit" 
3. Change price
4. Save

## Business Tips

### The 30-Day Rule
Items sitting 30+ days get flagged yellow. Consider:
- Lowering price
- Moving to " discount" area
- Donating (mark as "donated" in app)

### Zone Flow (Keep Things Moving)
**Goal:** Items should flow 1→2→3→4→5→6→7→8→9

**Don't let items sit in Zone 4 (cleaning) forever.**

### End of Day Routine (2 minutes)
1. Check dashboard
2. See what's newly sold
3. Verify Zone 9 items are actually picked up
4. Close app
