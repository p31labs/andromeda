## 2. Clinical Paradigm

### 2.1 Pathophysiology
Hypoparathyroidism is a rare endocrine disorder characterized by absent or inappropriately low PTH, resulting in chronic hypocalcemia (current: 7.8 mg/dL), hyperphosphatemia, hypercalciuria, and elevated risk of nephrocalcinosis, nephrolithiasis, and CKD.

### 2.2 Conventional Therapy Limitations
Standard treatment (high-dose oral calcium + calcitriol) fails to regulate renal calcium reabsorption, causing progressive renal and neurological damage over decades. Current serum calcium of 7.8 mg/dL is at the floor of acceptable targets, exposing the patient to tetany and cardiac arrhythmia risk.

### 2.3 Yorvipath Mechanism
A long-acting prodrug of teriparatide (PTH 1-34) providing continuous 24-hour PTH replacement. Targets root pathophysiology rather than downstream mineral supplementation. Enables reduction/elimination of conventional calcium and vitamin D dependence.

## 5. Pharmacological Logistics — Yorvipath Procurement

### 5.1 Prior Authorization Pathway (CareSource)
- **Drug:** Yorvipath (palopegteriparatide) — non-preferred specialty biologic
- **Prescriber:** Must be endocrinologist or specialist in consultation
- **Threshold:** Albumin-corrected serum Ca ≥ 7.8 mg/dL (ionized Ca ≥ 1.0 mmol/L)
- **Window:** Within 2 weeks prior to initiation
- **Risk:** Current Ca is exactly 7.8 — any drift below triggers automated denial

### 5.2 Critical Lab Strategy
- Maintain current calcium and calcitriol regimen strictly before qualifying draw
- Order MUST include serum calcium + serum albumin (BMP alone may omit albumin)
- Corrected Ca = measured Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 − serum albumin)

### 5.3 Ascendis APAP Denial Exception (Fallback)
If PA is denied:
1. Appeal through internal CareSource process + external state fair hearing
2. Once formally denied, Ascendis Patient Assistance Program (APAP) exception is triggered for Medicaid beneficiaries
3. Contact: 1-844-442-7236 (Ascendis Signature Access Program)
4. Result: Medication at $0 for eligible underinsured patients
